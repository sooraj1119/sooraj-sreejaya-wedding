# Royal Wedding Reception Digital Invitation (Sooraj & Sreejaya)

A luxury, interactive, scroll-driven digital wedding reception invitation experience featuring continuous cinematic video scrubbing.

## Highlights
- **Progressive Scroll Storytelling**: The romantic video of Sooraj & Sreejaya unfolds gracefully as guests scroll.
- **6 Themed Chapters**:
  1. The Beginning (Sooraj & Sreejaya wedding reception invitation)
  2. The Union (Family lineages: Son of Mr. Ajith Kumar & Mrs. Bindu Gopal, Daughter of Mr. Lakshmanan & Mrs. Nandini)
  3. The Celebration (Evening ambiance, cocktails, and music)
  4. The Gathering (Reception itinerary: Welcome, Grand Entry, Cake Cutting, First Dance, Gala Banquet)
  5. The Blessings (Best Compliments from Manjunath & Saritha and loved ones)
  6. The Invitation (Quick action buttons to view full details and RSVP)
- **High-Performance Frame Player**: 720 frames decoded into WebP sprite sheets for stutter-free 60fps/120fps scrubbing on both desktop and mobile devices.
- **Background Music**: Autoplays on user scroll or touch interaction with fallback prompt.
- **Reception Schedule & Calendar Sync**: View detailed timeline and download `wedding.ics` with one click.
- **Interactive RSVP**: Save attendance and warm wishes directly to local storage.

## How to Run
Run the local high-performance server:
```bash
python server.py 8081
```
Then open `http://localhost:8081/` in your browser.
