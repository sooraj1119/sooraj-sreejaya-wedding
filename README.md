# Royal Hindu Wedding Digital Invitation

A luxury, interactive, scroll-driven digital wedding invitation experience tailored for traditional Hindu weddings.

## Highlights
- **Progressive Scroll Storytelling**: Details, timings, and events reveal as guests scroll.
- **Lord Ganesha Invocation & Royal Motifs**: Authentic aesthetic with golden mandalas, paisley patterns, and traditional colors.
- **Interactive Event Timeline**: Haldi, Mehendi & Sangeet, Baraat, Shubh Vivah, and Reception.
- **Live Countdown Timer**: Counts down to the Shubh Muhurtham.
- **Venue & Google Maps Integration**: One-click directions for guests.
- **RSVP & Blessings Guestbook**: Guests can confirm attendance and leave heartfelt wishes.
- **Falling Petal Shower & Traditional Music Toggle**: Ambient ceremonial atmosphere.
- **Mobile-First Design**: Optimized for guests opening invites directly on smartphones via WhatsApp.

## How to Run & Customize
1. Open \index.html\ directly in any browser, or use VS Code Live Server / Vite.
2. Update the couple's names, dates, venues, and family details in \index.html\ or \script.js\.
